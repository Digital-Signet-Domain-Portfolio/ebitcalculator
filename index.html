EBIT (earnings before interest and taxes) measures a company's operating profit before financing costs and income tax. Compute EBIT three ways: from the top (Revenue minus COGS minus operating expenses), from the middle (Operating Income plus non-operating items), or from the bottom (Net Income plus Interest expense plus Income tax provision)[FASB ASC][SEC].
