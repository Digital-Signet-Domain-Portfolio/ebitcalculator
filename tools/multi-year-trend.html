Multi-year EBIT trend

The multi-year trend mode loads one walker column per year and renders the EBIT margin trajectory underneath. YoY deltas colour sage on positive and red on negative. Use for board-prep trend slides where the audience wants the trajectory in one frame[FASB ASC].

Input grid (illustrative)

LineFY22FY23FY24FY25
Revenue350,000365,000383,000391,000
Operating income108,000114,000118,000123,000
Interest expense2,9313,9334,2004,100
Tax provision19,30019,00029,70029,749
EBIT margin30.9%31.2%30.8%31.5%

Sage = above prior year, red = below.

YoY delta convention

Computed as (current year − prior year) / prior year. Margin deltas are absolute (percentage-point change), not relative.

Export

Trend grid can be copied to clipboard or exported as CSV via the CSV export page.
