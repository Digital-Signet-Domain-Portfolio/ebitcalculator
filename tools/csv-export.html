CSV export

The EBIT walker exports a clean CSV with one row per line and one column per scenario or year. Ungated: no email, no form. RFC 4180 compliant, so it loads correctly in Excel, Google Sheets, and Numbers without a delimiter prompt[RFC 4180].

CSV format

Line,FY25
Net Income,142.3
Interest expense,8.2
Income tax provision,47.6
Operating Income,198.1
Non-operating items,13.3
EBIT,211.4
D&A,36.5
EBITDA,247.9

Excel drop-in

Open in Excel, select the column, paste into your board pack. No conversion prompts because the file uses standard comma delimiters and proper line endings (CRLF for Excel-on-Windows compatibility, LF for everything else, auto-detected).

Why ungated

The analyst is at 11pm on a Wednesday. A form would be hostile. The CSV is the deliverable.
