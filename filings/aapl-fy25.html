Apple FY25 EBIT walkthrough

Apple's FY2025 EBIT was $133,050 million on revenue of $416,161 million, an EBIT margin of 32.0%. FY2026: $158,063 million (33.0%). Source: Apple's 10-K filed with the SEC on 3 November 2026[10-K].

Apple nets interest income, interest expense and investment gains into one line, "Other income/(expense), net", and does not report interest expense separately, so its EBIT here is operating income. Pretax income sits within about $1.2 billion of it in both years.

Check it from net income

EBIT on this site is net income plus interest expense plus the income tax provision, which is the same as operating income plus every non-operating item except interest expense (see EBIT from net income). The same figure from the bottom of the statement:

  • FY2026 (to 26 Sep 2026): 132,175 + 27,062 = 159,237 pretax; Apple does not report interest expense separately, so EBIT is taken at operating income, 158,063.
  • FY2025 (to 27 Sep 2025): 112,010 + 20,719 = 132,729 pretax; Apple does not report interest expense separately, so EBIT is taken at operating income, 133,050.

Sourcing notes

Every figure is from Apple's 10-K filed 3 November 2026 (SEC CIK 0000320193), which reports both fiscal years shown, read from the filing and its XBRL data on 16 November 2026. EBIT, the margins and EBITDA are computed from those lines. The filing is at SEC EDGAR.
