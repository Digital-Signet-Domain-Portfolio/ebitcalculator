NVIDIA FY25 EBIT walkthrough

NVIDIA's FY2025 EBIT was $84,273 million on revenue of $130,497 million, an EBIT margin of 64.6%. FY2026: $141,709 million (65.6%). Source: NVIDIA's 10-K filed with the SEC on 25 February 2026[10-K].

NVIDIA carries almost no interest expense ($247 million in FY2025), so nearly all of the gap between operating income and EBIT is interest income and other investment gains.

Check it from net income

EBIT on this site is net income plus interest expense plus the income tax provision, which is the same as operating income plus every non-operating item except interest expense (see EBIT from net income). The same figure from the bottom of the statement:

  • FY2026 (to 25 Jan 2026): 120,067 + 21,383 tax + 259 interest expense = 141,709.
  • FY2025 (to 26 Jan 2025): 72,880 + 11,146 tax + 247 interest expense = 84,273.

Sourcing notes

Every figure is from NVIDIA's 10-K filed 25 February 2026 (SEC CIK 0001045810), which reports both fiscal years shown, read from the filing and its XBRL data on 16 November 2026. EBIT, the margins and EBITDA are computed from those lines. The filing is at SEC EDGAR.
