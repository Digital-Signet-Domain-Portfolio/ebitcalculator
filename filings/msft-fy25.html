Microsoft FY25 EBIT walkthrough

Microsoft's FY2025 EBIT was $126,012 million on revenue of $281,724 million, an EBIT margin of 44.7%. FY2026: $168,985 million (50.9%). Source: Microsoft's 10-K filed with the SEC on 29 July 2026[10-K].

Microsoft reports interest expense inside "Other income (expense), net" and breaks it out in the notes, so EBIT adds it back on top of pretax income. In FY2025 the other-income line was a net loss of $4,901 million, which is why EBIT ($126,012 million) sits below operating income ($128,528 million) that year.

Check it from net income

EBIT on this site is net income plus interest expense plus the income tax provision, which is the same as operating income plus every non-operating item except interest expense (see EBIT from net income). The same figure from the bottom of the statement:

  • FY2026 (to 30 Jun 2026): 133,749 + 32,185 tax + 3,051 interest expense = 168,985.
  • FY2025 (to 30 Jun 2025): 101,832 + 21,795 tax + 2,385 interest expense = 126,012.

Sourcing notes

Every figure is from Microsoft's 10-K filed 29 July 2026 (SEC CIK 0000789019), which reports both fiscal years shown, read from the filing and its XBRL data on 16 November 2026. EBIT, the margins and EBITDA are computed from those lines. The filing is at SEC EDGAR.
