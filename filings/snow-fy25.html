Snowflake FY25 EBIT walkthrough

Snowflake reports a GAAP operating loss but a positive non-GAAP operating income (adjusted EBIT) after adding back stock-based compensation (including employer payroll taxes on stock-comp), amortization of acquired intangibles, acquisition-related expenses, and restructuring. The SBC add-back is by far the largest single line and is the canonical SaaS adjusted-EBIT example[EDGAR].

On an operating-margin basis the Rule-of-40 score is ~29% revenue growth (revenue rose from $2,806.5M in FY24) plus a ~6% non-GAAP operating (adjusted EBIT) margin, about 35, i.e. below 40. The Rule of 40 for SaaS is more often measured with free-cash-flow margin than operating margin.
